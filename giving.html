Frequently Asked Questions

Are gifts to Landmark tax-deductible?

Landmark Church is a tax-exempt nonprofit organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. Because every financial situation is different, please consult a qualified tax or financial advisor about your specific gift.

Will I receive a receipt and annual giving statement?

Yes. A receipt is emailed after each completed transaction, and Landmark provides an annual giving statement each year for contributions recorded with your donor information. Please keep your email and mailing address current in Church Center so your records reach you.

How does a stock gift work?

Select “Give Stock” to begin a secure, guided transfer. You will be asked to identify your brokerage account and the shares you would like to donate, then guided through the transfer to Landmark. Gifts of appreciated securities may offer tax advantages, but the outcome depends on your circumstances and the applicable tax rules.

What is a donor-advised fund?

A donor-advised fund, often called a DAF, is a charitable account held by a sponsoring organization. You can recommend that the sponsor send a grant from that account to Landmark. In general, any charitable deduction is associated with the original contribution to the DAF rather than the later grant recommendation, so a DAF grant does not create a second deduction.

Which giving option should I use?

Use Church Center for debit or credit cards, bank transfers, recurring gifts, and access to your giving history. Choose “Give Stock” or “Give from a DAF” for those types of gifts. You may also give by mail or in person if that is more convenient.

Who can help if I have a question?

For help with a stock or DAF transfer, Landmark’s records, annual statements, employer matching, or another type of gift, contact the church office.
